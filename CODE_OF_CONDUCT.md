# Contributor code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Critique ideas and code, and accept constructive feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Report concerns through the private contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban contributors for violations. The maintainer will consider context, protect reporter privacy where possible, and explain moderation decisions to the affected participants.
