# Contact the maintainers

Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/thoriqakbar0/redakt/issues) to ask a question, request a feature, or report a bug. For bugs, include your redakt version, operating system, and the command you ran. Use synthetic data to show what you expected and what redakt returned.

For a potential vulnerability, follow the [security policy](https://github.com/thoriqakbar0/redakt/blob/main/SECURITY.md) and use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/thoriqakbar0/redakt/security/advisories/new). If private reporting is unavailable, open an issue asking for a private channel and leave out vulnerability details. Keep customer information, credentials, and real logs out of public reports.
